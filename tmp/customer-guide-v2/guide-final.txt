1. What CareChart does 
CareChart brings common outpatient hospital tasks into one hospital-scoped workspace. It connects 
patient registration, appointments, the doctor's queue, clinical documentation, diagnostic orders, 
patient records, documents, billing, and audit history.

Billing Generate invoices and record payments, refunds, and voids.

2. Sign in, passwords, and the active hospital 
Sign in 
1. Open the staff sign-in page. 
2. Enter the email address assigned to your individual account. 
3. Enter your password and select Sign in. 
4. If you cannot remember the password, use Forgot password? and follow the approved reset 
process. 
 
Figure 2. Staff sign-in screen using demonstration credentials. 
Password rule: A new password must contain at least 8 characters, including at least one letter and one 
number. Never share a password or use one account for several people.

Active hospital 
The hospital shown at the top of the screen is the active hospital. Data entered or viewed on that screen 
belongs to that hospital workspace. 
• Super Admin users can open Active Hospital and switch between hospital accounts they manage. 
• Other staff see only the hospital or hospitals to which they are assigned. 
• Always confirm the hospital name before registering a patient, changing settings, or creating an 
invoice. 
Important: Changing the active hospital changes the workspace. It does not move patients, visits, staff, 
invoices, or configuration between hospitals. 
3. Roles and access 
CareChart shows only the menus allowed for the signed-in role. An administrator should give each 
person the smallest role needed for their job. 
Role Typical access 
Super Admin All hospital workspaces, hospital creation, active-hospital switching, and all operational 
areas. 
Admin / Clinic Admin Hospital administration, settings, records, billing, diagnostics, documents, and audit. 
Doctor Schedule, Doctor's Desk, records, diagnostics, and documents. 
Front Desk / Reception Patient registration, schedule, records, billing support, and documents. 
Nurse Schedule, patient records, and documents. 
Billing Billing and patient-record lookup needed for finance work. 
Lab / Radiology Diagnostics, patient-record lookup, and documents needed for report workflows. 
Patient The separate patient portal connected only to that patient's account. 
Role changes: Role changes take effect for future access. Remove or suspend access promptly when a 
person leaves or no longer needs the system.

4. Front Desk: find or register a patient 
Find an existing patient 
1. Open Front Desk. 
2. Search using the MRN, patient name, or phone number. 
3. Check more than one detail before selecting a result. This helps prevent choosing the wrong person. 
4. If no correct record exists, select Register new patient. 
 
Figure 3. Front Desk search before a patient is selected. 
Register a new patient 
1. Enter the patient's full name exactly as provided. 
2. Enter date of birth when available; confirm that the calculated age is correct. 
3. Choose gender and the doctor responsible for the visit. 
4. Enter phone, email, blood group, emergency contact, and address when collected under hospital 
policy. 
5. Add known allergies carefully. Press Enter after each allergy so it is saved as a separate item. 
6. Review the information with the patient, then select Save patient & continue to visit.

5. Appointments and check-in 
Book from the schedule 
1. Open Schedule and choose the required date. 
2. Select New appointment. 
3. Search for and select the patient. 
4. Choose the doctor, time, visit duration, and reason. 
5. Select Book appointment. 
6. When the patient arrives, open the appointment and complete check-in so the visit reaches the 
doctor's queue.

• The doctor or authorized team reviews the requested date and time before confirmation. 
• After confirmation, Front Desk checks the patient in when they arrive.

6. Doctor's Desk and waiting room 
Doctor's Desk brings together appointment requests, confirmed online appointments, and patients 
waiting for consultation. 
1. Review any appointment requests awaiting action. 
2. Confirm or decline according to the hospital's booking process. 
3. Open a patient from Waiting Room only when ready to begin the consultation. 
4. Confirm the patient and visit reason before documenting anything.

7. Document a clinical visit 
Complete the consultation 
1. Confirm the patient header and review any allergy alert before prescribing. 
2. Read the chief complaint and record measured vital signs. 
3. Enter examination findings or clinical feedback. 
4. Record the diagnosis and advice given to the patient. 
5. Add medicines and tests when clinically appropriate. 
6. Create a tracked diagnostic order when the lab or radiology team must receive and process the 
request. 
7. Save a draft if the visit is not ready. Otherwise confirm the signature checkbox and select Complete 
& sign visit. 
 
Figure 8. Consultation form using demonstration text only. 
Clinical responsibility: CareChart records information and displays alerts. It does not replace professional 
judgement. The signing clinician remains responsible for accuracy, appropriateness, and follow-up.

9. Hospital and staff administration 
Create a hospital account - Super Admin 
1. Open Admin and confirm the active hospital shown in the header. 
2. Under Create Hospital Account, enter the hospital or clinic name. 
3. Enter the first administrator's name and email. 
4. Set a temporary password that follows the password rule. 
5. Select Create hospital. 
6. Open Active Hospital and switch into the new hospital before configuring it. 
 
Figure 11. Admin page for hospital creation, staff invitations, and user management. 
Invite or add staff 
• Invite by email when the person should create their own password through the approved invitation 
process. 
• Add user manually only when the hospital's onboarding process requires an administrator-created 
account. 
• Choose the role carefully. The role controls which menus and records the person can access. 
• Administrator email and password fields should be entered manually; they must not copy the 
signed-in user's credentials automatically.

4. Never reuse a former employee's account for a new employee. 
10. Billing, invoices, and payments 
Generate and review invoices 
1. Complete and sign the clinical visit. 
2. Open Billing and choose the completed visit or invoice. 
3. Review the patient, invoice status, date, line items, unit price, discount, tax, and total. 
4. If a price is zero unexpectedly, stop before collecting money and configure the matching service 
price under Settings. 
5. Save changes only after the invoice is correct.

11. Diagnostics: collection, report upload, and doctor review 
Lab or radiology team 
1. Open Diagnostics and filter by type or status when needed. 
2. For a blood test, begin the collection process when the sample is actually received. For imaging, 
begin processing according to the department workflow. 
3. Complete the test or imaging work in the hospital's approved laboratory or radiology system. 
4. When the final report is ready, attach the approved PDF, JPEG, or PNG file to the in-progress order. 
5. Select Upload report & send to doctor. The order becomes ready for clinical review. 
 
Figure 16. Diagnostics page using fictional orders, patient, clinician, and report name. 
Ordering clinician 
1. Open the completed order. 
2. Open and read the attached report. 
3. When clinically satisfied, acknowledge the report using the available review control. 
4. Arrange any required patient follow-up outside the status change itself. 
Status meaning: Completed means a report was uploaded. Reviewed means an authorized clinician 
opened and acknowledged it. A status is not a substitute for reading the report.

12. Patient documents 
1. Open Documents. 
2. Search for the patient and verify the correct record. 
3. Choose an approved PDF, JPEG, or PNG file. 
4. Link it to a diagnostic order when the document is the final report for that order; otherwise keep it 
as a general patient document. 
5. Select Upload securely. 
6. Authorized staff can later open or download the document within the same hospital workspace. 
 
Figure 17. Patient Documents search screen. 
Before uploading: Confirm the patient, document type, and hospital. Do not upload unrelated files, 
passwords, exports containing multiple patients, or documents not approved by hospital policy.

13. Audit history 
Audit history is an append-only record of important security and workflow events for the active hospital. 
It can show the time, action, resource, user or system identifier, and reason when one was supplied. 
 
Figure 18. Audit history using demonstration timestamps and identifiers. 
• Use audit history for authorized operational, privacy, security, and support reviews. 
• Do not put passwords, tokens, or unnecessary clinical details into an audit reason. 
• An audit event shows that an action occurred; it does not by itself explain whether the action was 
clinically appropriate.

14. Configure a hospital in the recommended order 
Start here: Switch to the correct active hospital before every configuration step. Configuration belongs 
only to that hospital workspace. 
Step 1 - Locations and departments 
1. Open Settings. 
2. Enter the clinic name, a unique clinic code, address, and IANA timezone such as Asia/Kolkata. 
3. Select Add clinic. 
4. Choose the clinic, select Department, enter the department name, and add the configuration. 
5. Repeat for each real care location and department. 
 
Figure 19. Clinic, location, and department configuration. 
Step 2 - Practitioners and weekly schedules 
1. Select the doctor account and the clinic where the doctor works. 
2. Choose the department and enter specialty, qualification, and registration number when required. 
3. Select Save practitioner. 
4. Choose the practitioner and weekday, then enter start time, end time, and slot length. 
5. Add a break only when both break start and break end are entered. 
6. Select Save Weekly Schedule. Saving the same practitioner and weekday updates the existing entry 
instead of creating a duplicate. 

CareChart Customer Guide  |  Configuration and daily use 
Page 23 
 
Figure 20. Practitioner profile and weekly schedule configuration. 
Step 3 - Blocked time and holidays 
• Blocked practitioner time is for leave, meetings, or another period when one practitioner is 
unavailable. 
• Clinic holiday is for a date when the selected clinic is closed. 
• Enter a clear reason or holiday name so staff understand why slots are unavailable. 
Step 4 - Service catalog, prices, and tax 
1. Choose the clinic. 
2. Enter a stable service code and service name that match how the service will appear on invoices. 
3. Choose the category, such as Consultation, Medicine, Test, or Imaging. 
4. Enter the clinic price. Mark Taxable service only when approved by the hospital's finance process. 
5. Select Save service and price. 
6. If tax applies, choose the clinic, enter the tax name and rate, set the effective date, and add the tax 
rule.

15. Public booking and patient portal 
Public appointment booking 
• The public page can link a patient to the appointment request form. 
• The patient chooses an available hospital, clinic, doctor, date, and time according to the deployed 
booking workflow. 
• A request enters the review queue and becomes confirmed only after authorized review. 
Patient portal 
• The patient uses the separate patient portal entry point, not the staff sign-in form. 
• Activation must follow the hospital's identity-verification process.

16. New hospital setup checklist 
• Hospital account created and selected as the active hospital 
• Clinic locations, codes, addresses, and timezones entered 
• Departments and appointment types created 
• Doctor accounts linked to practitioner profiles 
• Every working weekday, slot length, and optional break checked 
• Planned leave, blocked periods, and clinic holidays entered 
• Common consultations, medicines, tests, and imaging services priced for each clinic 
• Tax rules reviewed and approved by the hospital's finance owner 
• Staff invited with the smallest role required 
• Email delivery, document storage, encryption, malware scanning, backups, and HTTPS tested 
• A complete fictional-patient workflow tested before entering real patient information 
Go-live approval: Technical availability does not replace hospital approval. Clinical, operational, privacy, 
legal, security, and finance owners should approve the configured workflow before real use.

17. Daily workflow checklists 
Walk-in visit 
1. Front Desk searches for or registers the patient. 
2. Front Desk logs the visit and selects the doctor. 
3. Doctor opens the patient from Waiting Room. 
4. Doctor documents, orders, and signs the visit. 
5. Lab or radiology processes any tracked order and uploads the final report. 
6. Ordering clinician reviews and acknowledges the report. 
7. Billing reviews prices, generates the invoice, and records payment. 
Online appointment 
1. Patient submits a public appointment request. 
2. Authorized staff or doctor reviews and confirms it. 
3. Front Desk checks the patient in on arrival. 
4. Clinical, diagnostic, record, and billing steps follow the same controlled workflow as a walk-in visit. 
18. Privacy, security, and safe documentation 
• Use an individual account. Never share passwords or leave a signed-in device unattended. 
• Confirm the active hospital and patient before viewing, entering, uploading, printing, or billing 
information. 
• Access only the minimum information needed for assigned work. 
• Do not include patient information in training material, sales documents, screenshots, support 
messages, or presentations unless formally approved and securely de-identified. 
• Use fictional identities for demonstrations. A common-looking name is not automatically de-
identified. 
• Remove staff emails, internal IDs, tokens, and environment details from customer-facing 
screenshots. 
• Use approved storage, encryption, malware scanning, backups, HTTPS, access review, incident 
response, and retention procedures. 
• Follow applicable hospital policy and legal/regulatory requirements for the country and care setting. 
Screenshot rule: A customer guide should contain only screenshots that were deliberately prepared for 
demonstration. Cropping is not enough when the original file still contains hidden or out-of-frame 
sensitive pixels; use a flattened sanitized copy.

19. Troubleshooting 
Problem What to check 
Wrong hospital is shown Super Admin: open Active Hospital and switch. Other staff: confirm assignment to 
the correct hospital. 
A menu is missing Check the signed-in role. CareChart hides functions that are outside the role's 
access. 
Patient is not found Confirm the active hospital and search using another approved identifier. Do not 
create a duplicate without checking. 
Doctor is missing from 
scheduling Confirm an active doctor account is linked to a practitioner profile and clinic. 
No appointment slots appear Check weekday schedule, slot length, breaks, blocked time, holidays, and existing 
appointments. 
Same weekday appears twice Save the same practitioner and weekday again to update it. Ask support to review 
older duplicate data if it remains. 
Password is rejected Use at least 8 characters with at least one letter and one number. 
Invoice price is zero Create or correct the active service and clinic price in Settings, then follow the 
approved invoice-correction process. 
Report cannot be uploaded Confirm the order is in progress and the file is an approved PDF, JPEG, or PNG 
within the configured size limit. 
Doctor cannot acknowledge 
report 
Confirm the report is completed and the user is the ordering clinician or an 
authorized administrator. 
Page remains on Loading Refresh once. If application assets return errors, contact the 
implementation/support team with the page and time. 
Information safe to send support 
• Page name and approximate time of the issue 
• Your role and the hospital name shown in the header 
• The non-sensitive error message 
• Steps that led to the issue and whether it remains after a refresh 
• A screenshot only after patient information, credentials, tokens, emails, IDs, and internal URLs are 
removed

20. Plain-language glossary 
Term Meaning 
Active hospital The hospital workspace currently selected at the top of the screen. 
Appointment type A named kind of visit with a standard duration. 
Audit event A time-stamped record that an important action happened. 
Clinical indication The reason a test or imaging procedure is requested. 
Completed diagnostic 
order A final report was uploaded and is waiting for clinical review. 
Department A care grouping inside a clinic, such as General Medicine or Pediatrics. 
Encounter / visit The clinical record for one patient attendance or consultation. 
IANA timezone A standard location-based timezone name, such as Asia/Kolkata. 
MRN Medical Record Number - the patient's identifier inside one hospital. 
Organization The hospital-level workspace that separates one customer's data from another's. 
Practitioner profile The configuration linking a clinician account to clinic, department, and scheduling details. 
Reviewed diagnostic 
order An authorized clinician opened and acknowledged the uploaded report. 
Service catalog The hospital's list of billable services and clinic prices. 
Tenant scoped Restricted to one hospital workspace rather than shared across hospitals.
